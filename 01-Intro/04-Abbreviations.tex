\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\caption[Mathematical notation used in this research.]{Mathematical notation used in this research. Each symbol is defined where it first appears, and Table~\ref{tab:notation-methods} adds the symbols that only the equations of Chapter~\ref{ch:methodology} use.}
\label{tab:notation}
\begin{adjustbox}{max width=\linewidth, max totalheight=0.86\textheight, center}%
\small\setlength{\tabcolsep}{3.5pt}%
\begin{tabular}{lp{0.72\linewidth}}
\toprule
Symbol & Meaning \\
\midrule
$G_{\mathrm{approve}}$ & Directed endorsement graph from latest ERC-20 allowances between contracts (owner $\to$ spender), each counted up to its owner's balance \\
$G_{\mathrm{transfer}}$ & Directed transfer graph between contracts (sender $\to$ receiver; AWP input) \\
$|V|$ & Number of nodes in a reputation graph \\
$|E|$ & Number of directed edges in a reputation graph \\
$w_{ij}$ & Weight of the directed edge from node $i$ to node $j$ \\
$o_i$ & Out-strength of node $i$, $o_i=\sum_j w_{ij}$ (written $o(u)$, or $o_A(u)$ and $o_T(u)$ per layer, in Chapter~\ref{ch:methodology}) \\
$P$ & Transition matrix, $P_{ij}=w_{ij}/o_i$ for $o_i>0$; a dangling row is uniform in Equation~\eqref{eq:transition} and zero in Equation~\eqref{eq:transition-impl}, where its mass is redistributed separately \\
$G_{\mathrm{PR}}$ & Google matrix $dP^{\top}+(1-d)\,\mathbf{1}\mathbf{1}^{\top}/|V|$ (Equation~\eqref{eq:google-matrix}) \\
$\mathbf{r}$, $r_i$ & PageRank vector and its entry for node $i$; $\mathbf{r}^{(t)}$ is the iterate after $t$ power-iteration steps \\
$\boldsymbol{\pi}$ & Teleportation (restart) distribution: uniform for EndorseRank and C-PR, proportional to activeness $X_u$ for AWP, and the normalized allowance-walk score for S-PR \\
$d$ & PageRank damping factor (default $0.85$); the teleport probability is $1-d$ \\
$\varepsilon$ & Convergence tolerance of power iteration ($10^{-8}$, on the $\|\cdot\|_1$ change between iterates) \\
$I_{\max}$ & Iteration cap of power iteration ($300$) \\
$\lambda$ & Allowance-layer weight of the coupled operator C-PR ($\lambda=0.5$ primary; $0.25$ and $0.75$ sensitivity; at $\lambda=1$ and $\lambda=0$ one layer is walked alone) \\
$P^{(\lambda)}$ & Coupled transition matrix of C-PR with allowance-layer weight $\lambda$ (Equation~\eqref{eq:coupled-transition}) \\
$\alpha_A(u)$, $\alpha_T(u)$ & Weights of the allowance row and the transfer row in the coupled transition row of node $u$ \\
$k$ & Logistic time-decay steepness ($0.01$) \\
$t_0$ & Logistic time-decay midpoint ($180$ days) \\
$\Delta t$ & Age in days of a transfer or approval before the decay anchor ($T_{\mathrm{obs}}$; $t_1$ in W0) \\
$\sigma(\Delta t)$ & Logistic time weight of a transfer (AWP) or latest approval (EndorseRank) \\
$V(z)$ & Bounded value weight of AWP and EndorseRank on a US-dollar amount $z$, $2/(1+e^{-bz})-1$ with $b=\ln 3/m$, so that the median amount $m$ ($m_T=160$ dollars for transfers, $m_A=0.071$ dollars for capped allowances) gets $V=1/2$ \\
$U$ & Cap on one allowance in the allowance layer of C-PR and in the in-approve value ($16{,}000$ dollars) \\
$X_u$ & Activeness of address $u$, AWP's restart weight \\
$n$ & Number of contracts in a correlation (matched cohort $n=6{,}443$; spender cohorts $n=7{,}864$ in W0 and $7{,}915$ in W1) \\
$\rho$ & Spearman's rank correlation coefficient; in the discovery-order test, the share by which a crawl's visits grow before it recomputes its ordering ($1/50$) \\
$\tau$ & Kendall's rank correlation coefficient; every reported value is $\tau_b$ \\
$T_{\mathrm{obs}}$ & End of the score window of the same-window evaluation and score freeze of W1 (30~June~2026, 23:59:59 UTC) \\
$t_1$, $t_2$ & Score freeze of W0 (31~March~2026, 23:59:59 UTC) and end of its label window (30~June~2026) \\
$\mathcal{W}$ & Set of contracts in the evaluation cohort \\
$s(w)$ & Reputation score of contract $w$ under a given method \\
$p(w)$ & Proxy value for contract $w$ \\
$\mathcal{U}$ & Universe of a run of the discovery-order test, the contracts that every crawl of the run visits \\
$I_T$, $I_A$ & Importance of a contract in that test: the US dollars that user accounts transferred to it, and the balance-capped US dollars that they authorized it to spend (at most $U_{\mathrm{user}}=31{,}000$ dollars per account) \\
$H_q(I)$, $\mathrm{AUC}_{x}$ & Hot set, the share $q$ of the universe with the largest importance $I$, and the normalized area under a crawl's discovery curve over its first $x|\mathcal{U}|$ visits \\
C-PR$'$, $\mathrm{IB}'_U$; $D_1$, $D_2$ & A prime marks a walk or count computed on the contracts a crawl has read so far; decision contrasts of $\mathrm{AUC}_{10\%}$, C-PR$'$ minus the breadth-first crawl and C-PR$'$ minus the backlink count $\mathrm{IB}'_U$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}%
\end{adjustbox}
\end{table}
