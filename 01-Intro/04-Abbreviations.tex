\section*{Mathematical notation}
\label{sec:notation-table}

Table~\ref{tab:notation} gives the symbols that recur across the chapters. The full notation of the graphs, scores and statistics is in Table~\ref{tab:notation-methods} of Chapter~\ref{ch:methodology}.

\begin{table}[htbp]
\centering
\caption{Mathematical notation used in this research.}
\label{tab:notation}
\begin{adjustbox}{max width=\linewidth, max totalheight=0.9\textheight, center}%
\small\setlength{\tabcolsep}{3.5pt}%
\begin{tabular}{lp{0.72\linewidth}}
\toprule
Symbol & Meaning \\
\midrule
$G_{\mathrm{approve}}$ & Directed endorsement graph from the latest allowances of the five tokens, valued in US dollars (owner $\to$ spender) \\
$G_{\mathrm{transfer}}$ & Directed transfer graph of the five tokens, amounts in US dollars (sender $\to$ receiver; AWP input) \\
$|V|$, $|E|$ & Numbers of nodes and of directed edges in a reputation graph \\
$w_{ij}$ & Weight of the directed edge from node $i$ to node $j$ \\
$o_i$ & Out-strength of node $i$, $o_i=\sum_j w_{ij}$ (written $o(u)$, or $o_A(u)$ and $o_T(u)$ per layer, in Chapter~\ref{ch:methodology}) \\
$P$ & Transition matrix, $P_{ij}=w_{ij}/o_i$ for $o_i>0$; a dangling row is uniform in Equation~\eqref{eq:transition} and zero in Equation~\eqref{eq:transition-impl}, where its mass is redistributed separately \\
$G_{\mathrm{PR}}$ & Google matrix $dP^{\top}+(1-d)\,\mathbf{1}\mathbf{1}^{\top}/|V|$ (Equation~\eqref{eq:google-matrix}) \\
$\mathbf{r}$, $r_i$ & PageRank vector and its entry for node $i$; $\mathbf{r}^{(t)}$ is the iterate after $t$ power-iteration steps \\
$\boldsymbol{\pi}$ & Teleportation (restart) distribution: uniform for EndorseRank and C-PR, proportional to activeness $X_u$ for AWP and for the variant of EndorseRank with activity restarts, and the normalized allowance-walk score for S-PR \\
$d$ & PageRank damping factor (default $0.85$); the teleport probability is $1-d$ \\
$\varepsilon$ & Convergence tolerance of power iteration ($10^{-8}$, on the $\|\cdot\|_1$ change between iterates) \\
$I_{\max}$ & Iteration cap of power iteration ($300$) \\
$\lambda$ & Allowance-layer weight of the coupled operator C-PR ($\lambda=1$ and $\lambda=0$: one layer walked alone) \\
$P^{(\lambda)}$ & Coupled transition matrix of C-PR with allowance-layer weight $\lambda$ (Equation~\eqref{eq:coupled-transition}) \\
$\alpha_A(u)$, $\alpha_T(u)$ & Weights of the allowance row and the transfer row in the coupled transition row of node $u$ \\
$U$ & Cap on an allowance in the allowance layer of C-PR and in the in-approve value: an allowance above $U$ counts $U$; $U=20{,}000$ dollars, the 99th percentile of the US-dollar values of single transfers up to $t_1$, rounded to two significant digits \\
$k$, $t_0$ & Steepness and midpoint (days) of the logistic time decay \\
$\Delta t$ & Age in days of a transfer or approval \\
$\sigma(\Delta t)$ & Logistic time weight of a transfer (AWP) or latest approval (EndorseRank) \\
$V(z)$, $b$ & Bounded value weight of AWP and EndorseRank on a US-dollar amount $z$, $2/(1+e^{-bz})-1$ with slope $b_T=\ln 3/m_T$ for transfers and $b_A=\ln 3/m_A$ for allowances, so that an amount of $m_T$ or $m_A$ gets $V=1/2$ \\
$m_T$, $m_A$ & Medians, rounded to two significant digits, of the US-dollar amounts of the transfers up to $t_1$ ($200$ dollars) and of the finite latest allowances at $t_1$, valued at $t_1$ prices ($62$ dollars) (Section~\ref{sec:amount-revision}) \\
$X_u$ & Activeness of address $u$, AWP's restart weight \\
$n$ & Number of contracts in a cohort or a benchmark stage (Section~\ref{sub:cohorts}) \\
$\rho$ & Spearman's rank correlation coefficient \\
$\tau$ & Kendall's rank correlation coefficient; every reported value is $\tau_b$ \\
$\Delta\tau$ & Paired difference of two Kendall coefficients on the same resamples \\
$T_{\mathrm{obs}}$ & End of the observation window and score freeze of W1 (30~June~2026) \\
$t_1$, $t_2$ & Score freeze of W0 (31~March~2026) and end of its label window (30~June~2026) \\
$\mathcal{W}$ & Matched cohort: contracts that received non-zero approvals of the five tokens from at least three distinct owners, counted over the five tokens together, in the observation window; the registered analysis used the plan's cohort, approved on any token (Appendix~\ref{sec:map-registered}) \\
$\mathcal{C}$ & Contracts evaluated in one analysis: $\mathcal{W}$, or a spender or trader cohort of a holdout \\
$\mathbf{s}_M$ & Reputation score vector of method $M$ on the contracts evaluated \\
$\mathbf{p}_q$ & Value vector of same-window proxy $q$ on the matched cohort \\
$B$ & Number of paired bootstrap resamples \\
$\delta$ & Non-inferiority margin of rule~B \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}%
\end{adjustbox}
\end{table}
