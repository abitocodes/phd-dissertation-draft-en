$\sigma(\Delta t)$ & Logistic decay weight applied to transfer edges \\
$\rho$ & Spearman's rank correlation coefficient \\
$\tau$ & Kendall's rank correlation coefficient \\
$|E|$ & Number of directed edges in a reputation graph \\
$\mathcal{W}$ & Set of wallets in the evaluation cohort \\
