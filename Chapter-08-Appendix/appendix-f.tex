\label{ch:appendix-ethics}

\dissertationSubheading[sec:ethics-certificate]{Certificate}

This study uses public blockchain data only and involves no human participants (Section~\ref{sec:ethics}). The School of Computing Research Ethics Review Committee of the College of Science, Engineering and Technology (CSET), University of South Africa, reviewed it as a low-risk application and granted ethics approval on 9 November 2026 for five years (reference 18316).
