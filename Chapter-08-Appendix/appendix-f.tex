\section{Ethics Clearance Certificate}
\label{ch:appendix-ethics}

This study uses public blockchain data only and involves no human participants (Section~\ref{sec:ethics}). The School of Computing Research Ethics Review Committee of the College of Science, Engineering and Technology (CSET), University of South Africa, reviewed it as a low-risk application and granted ethics approval on 9~November~2026 for five years (reference 18316). The certificate is reproduced on the two pages that close this appendix.
