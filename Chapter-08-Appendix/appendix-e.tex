\section{The Registered Analysis}
\label{sec:map-registered}

The registered analysis is the one that the analysis plan and the registration of W1 fixed before the study extracted the data it judges (Appendix~\ref{sec:pipeline-stages}): amounts in raw token base units, every ERC-20 token, the value transform with $b=1$, scores from every event since 1~October~2023, and the plan's cohort of $27{,}844$ contracts. The revised analysis, with amounts in US dollars, five tokens and scores from the twelve months before each freeze, was defined after its results were known (Section~\ref{sec:amount-revision}), and the chapters report it. Table~\ref{tab:registered-vs-revised} sets the decision contrasts and verdicts of the registered analysis beside the same contrasts on the revised scores; its last four rows, degree contrasts computed after the labels were known, carry no verdict. The two columns reach the same verdicts except on R1, which holds on the revised scores and not in the registered analysis; only the verdicts of the registered column are outcomes of tests fixed before the study extracted the data they judge. The complete registered results, with every coefficient, interval and verdict, are published unchanged in \texttt{data/3-published-results-for-thesis/} with their digest, \texttt{results\_digest.md}.
