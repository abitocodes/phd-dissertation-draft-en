\section{The Registered Analysis}
\label{sec:map-registered}

The registered analysis is the one that the analysis plan and the registration of W1 fixed before the study extracted the data it judges (Appendix~\ref{sec:pipeline-stages}): amounts in raw token base units, every ERC-20 token, the value transform with $b=1$, scores from every event since 1~October~2023, graphs that hold every account of the cohort's events, and the plan's cohort of $27{,}844$ contracts. After the registered results were known, the revised analysis was defined: amounts in US dollars on five tokens; scores from the twelve months before each freeze; graphs whose nodes are contracts other than smart accounts, with edges read from the whole chain; each allowance counted up to its owner's balance at the freeze, with balances reconstructed from \texttt{Transfer} logs; constants set on the contract graph; and the discovery-order test. The chapters report this analysis (Section~\ref{sec:amount-revision}), in which rules~A and~B are applied to the scores of the contract graph as a re-analysis. Table~\ref{tab:registered-vs-revised} sets the decision contrasts and verdicts of the registered analysis beside the same contrasts on those scores; its last four rows, degree contrasts computed after the labels were known, carry no verdict. Of rule~B the thesis applies two of its registered criteria, F1 and F2; under the registration the rule holds only if each of its criteria holds. The registration is the file \texttt{docs/registration\_w1.md} of the public repository (Section~\ref{sec:reproducibility}). The two columns reach the same verdicts: neither part of rule~A is met, F1 holds and F2 fails, also in the two registered sensitivity analyses, so rule~B is not met, and F6 fails. Only the verdicts of the registered column are outcomes of tests fixed before the study extracted the data they judge. The complete registered results, with every coefficient, interval and verdict, are published unchanged in \texttt{data/3-published-results-for-thesis/} with their digest, \texttt{results\_digest.md}.
