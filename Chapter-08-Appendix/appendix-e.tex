\section{The Registered Analysis}
\label{sec:map-registered}

The registered analysis is the one that the analysis plan and the registration of W1 fixed before the study extracted the data it judges (Appendix~\ref{sec:pipeline-stages}): amounts in raw token base units, every ERC-20 token, the value transform with $b=1$, and the plan's cohort of $27{,}844$ contracts. The amounts were revised after its results were known (Section~\ref{sec:amount-revision}), and the chapters report the revised analysis. Table~\ref{tab:registered-vs-revised} sets the decision contrasts and verdicts of the registered analysis beside the same contrasts on the revised scores; its last four rows, degree contrasts computed after the labels were known, carry no verdict. Both columns reach the same verdicts, but only those of the registered column are outcomes of tests fixed before the study extracted the data they judge. The complete registered results, with every coefficient, interval and verdict, are published unchanged in \texttt{data/3-published-results-for-thesis/} with their digest, \texttt{results\_digest.md}.
