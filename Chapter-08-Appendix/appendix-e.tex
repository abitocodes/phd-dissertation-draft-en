\section{The Registered Analysis}
\label{sec:map-registered}

The analysis plan and the registration of W1 fixed amounts in raw token base units, every ERC-20 token, and the value transform with $b=1$; under that setting the matched cohort is the plan's cohort of $27{,}844$ contracts. The plan was committed before the study extracted any log, and the registration before it extracted the logs of July to September~2026 (Appendix~\ref{sec:pipeline-stages}). The amounts were revised after the results of that analysis were known (Section~\ref{sec:amount-revision}), and the chapters report the revised analysis.

Table~\ref{tab:registered-vs-revised} sets the decision contrasts and verdicts of the registered analysis beside the same contrasts on the revised scores. Its last four rows, the degree contrasts of both windows, were computed after the labels were known in both analyses and carry no verdict. Every verdict in the registered column is the outcome of a test fixed before the study extracted the data it judges; no verdict in the revised column is, because the rules were applied to scores defined after the registered results were known. The two columns reach the same verdicts. The complete registered results, with every coefficient, interval and verdict, are published unchanged in \texttt{data/3-published-results-for-thesis/} with their digest, \texttt{results\_digest.md}; the revised results are in its subfolder \texttt{revised-usd/}.
