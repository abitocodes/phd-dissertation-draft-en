\section{Claim-to-Evidence Map}
\label{ch:appendix-claims}

This appendix maps each hypothesis, research question, and empirical claim to the specific evidence in Chapter~\ref{ch:results}. It is intended as a navigation aid for examiners and replicators; numerical values are not repeated here.

\dissertationSubheading[sec:map-h1]{H1 / RQ1 --- Rank divergence}

\begin{itemize}
\item Statement: EndorseRank and AWP yield distinct wallet orderings on the same cohort.
\item Evidence: inter-method Kendall $\tau$ with 95\% CI (Table~\ref{tab:alignment-family-ci}, last row; Section~\ref{sec:rank-divergence}); rank scatter in Figure~\ref{fig:rank-scatter}; score distributions in Figure~\ref{fig:score-distributions}.
\item Interpretation: related but not interchangeable rankings; O1 criterion met (Table~\ref{tab:objectives-achievement}).
\end{itemize}

\dissertationSubheading[sec:map-h2]{H2 / RQ2 --- Construct-specific alignment}

\begin{itemize}
\item Statement: the two methods differ on contemporaneous allowance versus transfer checks.
\item Evidence: family means with CI (Table~\ref{tab:alignment-family-ci}); paired $\Delta\tau$ contrasts ii and iii (Table~\ref{tab:tau-diff}); per-proxy detail (Tables~\ref{tab:alignment-transfer} and~\ref{tab:alignment-allowance}); heatmap Figure~\ref{fig:alignment-heatmap}.
\item Qualification: within-method contrasts i and vi in Table~\ref{tab:tau-diff} include zero; the claim is between-method only.
\end{itemize}

\dissertationSubheading[sec:map-h3]{H3 / RQ3 --- Computational efficiency}

\begin{itemize}
\item Statement: EndorseRank is faster than AWP at equal $n$, attributable to edge count.
\item Evidence: Table~\ref{tab:benchmark-runtime} (runtime, SD, memory, iterations, $|V|$, $|E|$); Table~\ref{tab:benchmark-scaling-incohort}; Table~\ref{tab:benchmark-scaling}; Figure~\ref{fig:runtime-scaling}.
\item Qualification: expanded-pool stages add nodes without edges (Section~\ref{sub:runtime-scaling}); the claim does not cover edge growth.
\end{itemize}

\dissertationSubheading[sec:map-rq4]{RQ4 --- Temporal holdout}

\begin{itemize}
\item Statement: after scores are frozen at t1, EndorseRank predicts t2 new owner--spender approvals on inbound spenders more strongly than AWP.
\item Evidence: Table~\ref{tab:holdout-spenders} (Section~\ref{sec:gf-pr-ceiling}).
\item Qualification: the label is the endorsement construct in the future tense, not a credit or trading outcome; RQ4 replaced the proposal's trading-success comparison (Section~\ref{sub:gf-pr}).
\end{itemize}

\dissertationSubheading[sec:map-c1c4]{Claims C1--C4}

\begin{itemize}
\item C1: runtime evidence as in H3/RQ3.
\item C2: intended-construct checks---allowance for EndorseRank, transfer for AWP---in Table~\ref{tab:alignment-family-ci}.
\item C3: construct-specific winners as in H2/RQ2, tested in Table~\ref{tab:tau-diff}.
\item C4: efficiency--alignment trade-off combining C1, C3 and the spender holdout (Table~\ref{tab:holdout-spenders}); robustness in Tables~\ref{tab:robustness-damping}--\ref{tab:robustness-sample-size} and Figure~\ref{fig:damping-sensitivity}.
\end{itemize}

\dissertationSubheading[sec:map-nonclaims]{Explicit non-claims}

This research does \emph{not} claim:

\begin{itemize}
\item that AWP is obsolete;
\item that EndorseRank's allowance alignment exceeds its own transfer alignment;
\item that runtime scales favourably as the edge population grows beyond the cohort graph;
\item that results generalise beyond Arbitrum One and the study window without replication;
\item that on-chain scores replace collateral or legal identity systems.
\end{itemize}

\dissertationSubheading[sec:map-artifacts]{Artifact checklist for replication}

\begin{enumerate}
\item Merged wallet-ranking table --- scores for EndorseRank and AWP.
\item Machine-readable holdout summary for the spender cohort.
\item Machine-readable evaluation summary --- authoritative same-window numerics, per-run timings, bootstrap protocol.
\item Exported LaTeX table fragments (\texttt{results/tables/}).
\item Exported vector figures (\texttt{Figures/generated/}).
\item Optional archived extended-baseline matrices.
\end{enumerate}

Replicators can regenerate tables and figures by running the evaluation pipeline on real data with robustness sweeps, exporting the tables, and regenerating figures from the same artifacts (Appendix~\ref{sec:pipeline-stages}).
