\section{Data Description}
\label{ch:appendix-data}

This appendix describes the extracted data behind Chapter~\ref{ch:results}: the volume of the ego extraction and of the two graphs, the five tokens with their prices and allowance amounts, and the activity of GMX~V2 contract accounts by quarter.

\dissertationSubheading[sec:data-events]{Extracted events and graphs}

The ego extraction of the observation window was made for the plan's cohort of $27{,}844$ contracts. It holds $389{,}866{,}882$ approval logs, zero-value ones included, and $1{,}538{,}404{,}067$ transfer logs of non-zero value of every ERC-20 token, each with a contract of that cohort at one end. The revised analysis keeps the $224{,}954{,}202$ approvals and $1{,}149{,}168{,}538$ transfers that are of the five tokens and have a contract of the revised cohort at one end, $57.7\%$ and $74.7\%$ of the extracted logs; of the July--September logs of W1 it keeps $12{,}209{,}894$ approvals and $60{,}182{,}192$ transfers. At $T_{\mathrm{obs}}$ the pair tables built from the kept events hold $11{,}219{,}116$ allowance pairs and $25{,}018{,}892$ transfer pairs, and at $t_1$, $10{,}650{,}258$ and $23{,}680{,}692$. The $9{,}002$ and $17{,}676$ pairs at $T_{\mathrm{obs}}$ whose value weight is zero in double precision, and the $2{,}602$ and $2{,}246$ addresses that only they touch, are not part of the solver graphs of EndorseRank and AWP (Table~\ref{tab:benchmark-runtime}).

\dissertationSubheading[sec:data-tokens]{Tokens, prices and allowance amounts}

Of the $133$ Arbitrum One tokens that Chainlink's directory of data feeds maps, $64$ meet the feed rule of Section~\ref{sec:amount-revision} and occur in the data of the observation window, and $46$ of these have a daily price from DefiLlama \parencite{defillamanddocs}. The five that the analysis keeps hold $75.75\%$ of the rows of all tokens in the ego tables of the plan's cohort (transfers up to $T_{\mathrm{obs}}$ and positive latest allowances at $T_{\mathrm{obs}}$). Table~\ref{tab:usd-tokens} gives for each of them the address, decimals and feed, the rows held and kept, the US-dollar volume of the kept transfers and the data behind the daily prices; its caption counts the rows left out, those of the other tokens and those of the five tokens in which no contract of the revised cohort takes part.

\input{results/tables/usd-tokens.tex}

DefiLlama's series under the Arbitrum One address begins in August~2024 for WETH and WBTC and in January~2025 for USDT0, so their earlier days take the price of the same asset under its CoinGecko identifier (Table~\ref{tab:usd-tokens}), which differs from the Arbitrum series by less than $0.1\%$ in median on the days both cover. No day of the five tokens is filled from the previous day or replaced as a data error, and no kept transfer precedes its token's first price. Against the Chainlink feeds read on the chain on $38$ dates per token \parencite{chainlinknddatafeeds}, the DefiLlama prices differ by a median of $0.01\%$ to $0.08\%$ per token and by at most $0.72\%$, for WETH.

Unlimited allowances are common. Of the $12{,}590{,}613$ positive latest allowances at $T_{\mathrm{obs}}$, $5{,}763{,}757$ ($45.8\%$) are of $1.15\times10^{77}$ base units or more, close to $2^{256}-1$, and $6{,}776{,}289$ ($53.8\%$), the unlimited ones included, are worth at least the cap $U$. The $12{,}590{,}613$ allowances belong to $6{,}208{,}769$ owners, of whom $3{,}294{,}370$ hold at least one unlimited allowance and $797{,}852$ hold both an unlimited and a finite one. In the registered analysis, which kept every token in raw base units, $99.52\%$ of the ego transfers and $99.78\%$ of the $28{,}069{,}418$ latest allowances at $T_{\mathrm{obs}}$ were of ten base units or more, where $V$ with $b=1$ is within $10^{-4}$ of one, and $1{,}532{,}301$ of the $13{,}029{,}306$ owners with a positive latest allowance held both an unlimited and a smaller allowance.

Contract accounts closed the most positions in the second half of 2025 and far fewer in 2026. A trader cohort further requires the account to be a node of the graph at the freeze: of the $127$ contract accounts with at least three closes in April--June~2026, $88$ form the trader cohort of W0, and of the $131$ in July--September, $70$ form that of W1.
