\section{Data Description}
\label{ch:appendix-data}

This appendix describes the extracted data behind Chapter~\ref{ch:results}: the volume of the ego extraction and of the two graphs, the five tokens with their prices and allowance amounts, and the activity of GMX~V2 contract accounts by quarter.

\dissertationSubheading[sec:data-events]{Extracted events and graphs}

The ego extraction of scan~A, 1~October~2023 to 30~June~2026, was made for the plan's cohort of $27{,}844$ contracts. It holds $389{,}866{,}882$ approval logs, zero-value ones included, and $1{,}538{,}404{,}067$ transfer logs of non-zero value of every ERC-20 token, each with a contract of that cohort at one end. The revised analysis keeps, for each freeze, the approvals and transfers of the five tokens in its score window that have a contract of the freeze's cohort at one end (Section~\ref{sec:dataset} gives their number); of the July--September logs of W1 it keeps $12{,}200{,}682$ approvals and $58{,}625{,}425$ transfers. At $T_{\mathrm{obs}}$ the pair tables built from the kept events hold $3{,}810{,}253$ allowance pairs and $8{,}432{,}635$ transfer pairs, and at $t_1$, $4{,}117{,}103$ and $9{,}198{,}965$. The $1{,}304$ and $6{,}511$ pairs at $T_{\mathrm{obs}}$ whose value weight is zero in double precision, and the addresses that only they touch (Section~\ref{sec:dataset}), are not part of the solver graphs of EndorseRank and AWP (Table~\ref{tab:benchmark-runtime}).

\dissertationSubheading[sec:data-tokens]{Tokens, prices and allowance amounts}

Section~\ref{sec:amount-revision} gives the rule by which the five tokens were chosen. In the score window of $T_{\mathrm{obs}}$ they hold $79.40\%$ of the rows of all tokens in the ego tables of the plan's cohort (transfers in the window and positive latest allowances at $T_{\mathrm{obs}}$ set in it). Table~\ref{tab:usd-tokens} gives for each of them the address, decimals and feed, the rows held and kept, the US-dollar volume of the kept transfers and the data behind the daily prices; its caption counts the rows left out, those of the other tokens and those of the five tokens in which no contract of the revised cohort takes part.

\input{results/tables/usd-tokens.tex}

DefiLlama's series under the Arbitrum One address begins in August~2024 for WETH and WBTC and in January~2025 for USDT0, before the first score window opens, so every one of the $548$ days that the windows cover is priced under the token's own address. No day of the five tokens is filled from the previous day or replaced as a data error, and no kept transfer precedes its token's first price. Against the Chainlink feeds read on the chain on $20$ of these days per token, the DefiLlama prices differ by a median of $0.010\%$ to $0.056\%$ per token and by at most $0.24\%$, for WBTC.

Unlimited allowances are common. Of the $4{,}185{,}287$ positive latest allowances at $T_{\mathrm{obs}}$, $2{,}084{,}669$ ($49.8\%$) are of $1.15\times10^{77}$ base units or more, close to $2^{256}-1$, and $2{,}331{,}420$ ($55.7\%$), the unlimited ones included, are worth at least the cap $U$. The $4{,}185{,}287$ allowances belong to $2{,}508{,}872$ owners, of whom $1{,}389{,}569$ hold at least one unlimited allowance and $256{,}803$ hold both an unlimited and a finite one. In the registered analysis, which kept every token in raw base units, $99.52\%$ of the ego transfers and $99.78\%$ of the $28{,}069{,}418$ latest allowances at $T_{\mathrm{obs}}$ were of ten base units or more, where $V$ with $b=1$ is within $10^{-4}$ of one, and $1{,}532{,}301$ of the $13{,}029{,}306$ owners with a positive latest allowance held both an unlimited and a smaller allowance.

Contract accounts closed the most positions in the second half of 2025 and far fewer in 2026. A trader cohort further requires the account to be a node of the graph at the freeze: of the $127$ contract accounts with at least three closes in April--June~2026, $87$ form the trader cohort of W0, and of the $131$ in July--September, $70$ form that of W1.
