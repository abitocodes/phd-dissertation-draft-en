3At the sizes of a figure
8 mmblend, keyline, main lines
5 mmmedal: white on a disc
  • From about 10 mm a picture carries the blend, the keyline and four or five white lines.
  • At 8 mm the white lines thin to 0.2 mm, the least that prints; keep the two or three that name the object.
  • At 5 mm and below the blend no longer reads: use the medal, a flat disc of the meaning’s colour with the pictogram in white.
